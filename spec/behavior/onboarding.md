# Onboarding and Invites

This file covers the in-app onboarding behavior: the first-officer wizard, invite tokens, the new-member tutorial, and chapter-directory search. The signup/payment flow and chapter lifecycle are specced in [`../product/onboarding.md`](../product/onboarding.md) — cross-reference, not duplicated here.

## First-Officer Onboarding Wizard

- Fires on first sign-in when the authenticated user has **no chapter memberships** (`GET /v1/chapters` returns empty). **Web** mounts it as a full-screen overlay. **Mobile** is a deliberate route (`apps/mobile/app/(auth)/create-chapter.tsx`) reached from join's "I'm starting our chapter" answer (the chapter-picker empty state's button opens join), and from More when the account has zero memberships — so the wizard can own its lifecycle after submit flips the membership count to 1. `(auth)/_layout.tsx` exempts `/create-chapter` from the post-auth bounce into the tabs for that reason. Both surfaces read the archetype catalog from `@repo/org-archetypes` (`ARCHETYPES`). Web maps a directory row's archetype through `getArchetype`, which falls back to IFC; mobile asks instead when the row's archetype isn't one it knows (below); mobile declares that workspace package in `apps/mobile/package.json` rather than keeping a local catalog.
- **Web zero-membership is not create-only.** The overlay still opens (same trigger), but the find step's **I have an invite** control goes to `/join`. The dialog stays non-dismissable via Escape so a first officer cannot abandon mid-create by accident; `/join` is outside `DashboardShell`, so the overlay does not remount there. `/join` offers **Create a chapter** back to `/chat`, which remounts the wizard. Mobile already routes zero memberships to s02, which asks whether the member has an invite or is starting the chapter (#3350).
- **Web `/join` for a member who already has a chapter (#3850).** The page doesn't redirect members away in general: an invite link may be for a second chapter, the chapter switcher's **Join another chapter** opens it, and `/no-access` sends a member with no role here, who would loop back to `/no-access` from `/chat`. Instead it leads with **You're already in <chapter>** and **Open chat**, and keeps the invite form as the secondary action. A member `/no-access` would send back (no role in their only chapter, `isLockedOutOfChapter` in `apps/web/lib/auth/no-access.ts`) doesn't see it. The one exception is the sign-in footer's **Join your chapter**, which signs in toward `/join?from=sign-in`. A member who arrives that way with no invite in the URL, no invite emailed to them, and not locked out by that rule goes straight to `/chat`. The page decides once, on arrival, so a join made there never triggers it (`shouldSkipJoinForMember` in `apps/web/lib/auth/join-visit.ts`).
- **"No chapter memberships" is the only robust (re-)trigger signal.** A wizard-created membership is written with `has_completed_onboarding=true`, and the chapters-list response does not expose `enabled_modules`, so a client cannot detect "chapter still at the default seed" from it. Once opened, the wizard owns its own lifecycle — the membership count flipping to 1 mid-flow does not auto-close it.
- **Mobile's steps (#3351):** **Find** the chapter in the directory, **Confirm** the match (a card, its fields behind `Edit details`) or describe it by hand, then pick its **Color** and accept the Terms. The archetype comes from the directory match; it is asked only on manual entry, or when the match's archetype is unknown to the app, with nothing preselected. For an organization that doesn't display its Greek letters (Phi Gamma Delta), neither the letters nor their switch appear in setup, the directory results leave them off, and the chapter is sent with the switch off. Both wizards decide it the same way: by the picked directory row's organization, which holds whatever the name field says later, else by the name as typed, alone or with more ("FIJI", "Phi Gamma Delta Tau Nu", `orgHidingGreekLettersIn`). Naming such an organization, by its row or by hand, turns the switch off and fills the short name only when the founder left it empty (`orgMarkFieldsFor`). Either way the letters are still stored, hidden ([`branding.md`](branding.md) § Chapter mark: the switch hides, it doesn't delete). Create opens `<name> is live` with the seeded channels and an optional logo upload, then `Pick your chat display` (#4056: the founder's own display, as s03 asks a joining member), then the `Turn on notifications` step (only when the build can push and push is off), then the invite step (only when the new chapter is active), then chat. The invite step never shows a token: each press of **Share invite** mints one invite (`POST /v1/invites`, the chapter's Member role) and opens the platform share sheet with its join link, so one press sends one person one link. Nothing is minted before a press, so skipping the step leaves no invite. On iOS a sheet closed without sending keeps its link for the next press; Android can't report that, so there every opened chooser counts as sent. Links sent are listed with whether each has been used, read by polling `GET /v1/invites` every 15 seconds while the step is open and a link is out. A link counts as used when its row's `status` is `joined`; a revoked one also carries `used_at`, so `used_at` is read only from a row with no `status` (an API before #3652). The logo and invite routes act on the active chapter, so when the new chapter couldn't be made active the live step names no channels and offers no logo, and setup ends at chat with no invite step. All of it is one route, because the gates exempt `/create-chapter` by exact path.
- Submit calls `POST /v1/chapters/onboard` on the **cold path** (NestJS + service-role Supabase), not the chat hot path. `ChapterOnboardingService.onboard`:
  1. Materializes the chapter config from the chosen archetype, **deep-copying the archetype seed** so per-chapter edits never mutate the shared reference. Sets `org_archetype`, `enabled_modules`, `vocabulary`, and a derived `theme_palette`. The client never supplies the module map — it is resolved server-side from the archetype.
  2. Creates the chapter with branding + `directory_id` (when matched in the directory), default roles, the creator's President membership, and the default channels seeded from `DEFAULT_CHANNELS` (the set, and each channel's type and gating: [`chat/README.md` § Channels](chat/README.md#channels)). The no-channels empty state names no seeded channel (web `Onboarding seeds them. Ask an admin.`, mobile `Ask an admin to add one.`), so it never lists `#alumni` to someone who can't see it. Copy that lists channels by name, such as mobile chapter setup's live step, names only the three public ones.
  3. Seeds the archetype's default custom fields into `chapter_custom_fields`, so Settings → Fields opens populated rather than empty. Best-effort and idempotent; the key derivation and skip rules live with the rest of the Fields-tab behavior in [`settings/customization.md`](settings/customization.md#fields-tab), not here.
  4. Posts a one-time welcome `system_audit` message into `#general` (resolved by name; skipped if absent): `"Welcome to the <designation> chapter of <name>. Invite your chapter to get the conversation started."` (for example "Welcome to the Tau Nu chapter of FIJI."). With only one of the two it reads `"Welcome to the <designation> chapter."` or `"Welcome to <name>."`, and with neither, `"Welcome to your chapter."`; the second sentence is the same in every case. A designation that ends in the word "chapter" drops it, so the sentence never says "chapter" twice. `<name>` is the short name the wizard set, else the chapter's name field (the organization's name on the directory path, free text on manual entry). It is never Greek letters, whatever the chapter's letters setting, because some organizations (Phi Gamma Delta among them) don't allow their letters to be shown: a name or designation with any Greek letter in it is left out. A name that repeats the designation ("FIJI Tau Nu") drops it, and one that is only the designation is left out (`welcomeGreeting`). Sent by the system user (drawn with the Frapp crest, [`chat/README.md`](chat/README.md#hot-path-client-behavior)); best-effort (a failure does not roll back chapter creation).
  5. Navigates the client. **Web goes to `/billing`**, not chat (#2297); mobile goes to `/` — chat home.
- **Web lands a new founder on checkout, because the chapter it just made cannot write anything.** `subscription_status` defaults to `'incomplete'` (`00000000000000_initial_schema.sql:34-35`) and `ChapterService.create` has no billing dependency, so a brand-new chapter is in the one state where every screen loads and every paid-ops **write** is refused. Landing on chat left the founder there with no explanation — the Guideline 2.1 finding in #2297.
  - **The gate is writes-only.** `ChapterGuard.enforceSubscription` returns before any status branch for `GET`/`HEAD`/`OPTIONS`, so reads are never refused and a lapsed or incomplete chapter can still see everything. Do not describe this gate as blocking events/tasks/points wholesale — it blocks *changing* them. Matrix: [`docs/guides/api-architecture.md`](../../docs/guides/api-architecture.md) § Subscription enforcement; read access is also stated in [`billing.md`](billing.md).
  - **Recorded decision (2026-09-18, owner):** make the `incomplete` state unreachable on the normal path rather than widen the free tier. `@FreeTier()` is **not** added to the paid-ops controllers — the boundary below stands as written. The founder is put in front of checkout instead, which costs nothing on day zero because #913's trial (`grantTrial: !chapter.subscription_id`) makes Stripe report `trialing`, which `mapStripeStatus` folds to `active`.
  - **A landing, not a gate.** Nothing blocks navigating away from `/billing`; the dashboard shell and its nav are fully available. That is what keeps this consistent with [`../product/positioning.md`](../product/positioning.md) § Officer onboarding, which requires ops setup to be an inline nudge rather than a mandatory gate.
  - **Checkout is web-only, and that is a store commitment, not a preference.** `apps/mobile/store/README.md` § Identity (the Price row) and § Review notes carry the submitted App Store Connect answers: the app has no in-app purchases and chapter subscriptions "are not offered, linked or mentioned in the app". Mobile's half of #2297 is therefore copy only — a refusal is explained and the retry withdrawn, naming no price, plan, purchase path or link. Adding one trades a 2.1 finding for a 3.1.1 one.
- **Manual-entry path:** when the officer's chapter is not in the directory, the wizard submits with no `directory_id`. The service writes a `chapter_directory_requests` row (status `pending`) so the curated directory seed can be backfilled later. The row is RLS-enabled and API-only (no client policies), like `chapter_directory`.
- **Actor identity** for the chapter, membership, welcome message, and directory request is **always resolved from the authenticated session**, never from the request payload. The `requested_by` on a directory request comes from the session.

## Invite Token Rules

- Tokens **expire after 24 hours**.
- A token can be used **only once** (`used_at` is set on redemption).
- An expired or already-used token returns **410 Gone**.
- Each token carries a `role` that determines the joining member's initial role. If that role was deleted between creation and redemption, the user is assigned the chapter's default "Member" role (`system_key` `MEMBER`) instead. A chapter with no such role, a rare legacy case ([`rbac.md`](rbac.md)), gets a member with no role, and the preview below reports its role as `null`. When the token's role is not a seeded one (`is_system` false: a role the chapter made, or one a Discord import brought over) and grants neither `members:view` nor `*`, the member also gets the chapter's Member role, listed after the token's role: such a role usually carries nothing but a channel read permission, and without `members:view` the member is refused on every screen. A chapter with no Member role grants the token's role alone. Every other role is granted alone.
- Only users with the `members:invite` permission can generate tokens. Multiple tokens can be generated at once (batch invite), or one per address via a bulk email send (`POST /v1/invites/email`) that mints a token per address and emails each a join link. Email delivery is best-effort per address — a send failure never invalidates the token, it is only reported back so the caller can retry or share the link manually.
- **Minting an invite is part of the free-tier wedge, but it is not ungated.** `InviteService.create` / `createBatch` / `createWithEmails` never check `subscription_status` themselves — the gate lives in `ChapterGuard`, via `@FreeTier()` on the controller class plus `@GraceBlocked()` on those three creating routes. So a brand-new `incomplete` chapter from the onboarding wizard **can** mint tokens, but a `past_due` chapter **cannot**: creation is refused with **403** (code `chapter.subscription.invite_blocked` in the response body) even inside the 3-day grace window that still permits its other free-tier writes, and a null `past_due_since` counts as within grace. A `canceled` chapter cannot mint either. Billing gates the paid ops modules more broadly than it gates chat/members/invites, but "free-tier" never means "always allowed". Full matrix: [`docs/guides/api-architecture.md`](../../docs/guides/api-architecture.md) § Subscription enforcement (ChapterGuard).
- **Redeeming is subscription-gated in the service, not the guard (#1546).** `POST /v1/invites/redeem` carries no `ChapterGuard`: `InviteController` applies `ChapterGuard`/`PermissionsGuard` per route and `redeem` declares neither. It is still authenticated — `SupabaseAuthGuard` is class-level. It _cannot_ simply be guarded, and not because the caller lacks chapter context (`ChapterGuard` resolves one per [`multi-tenancy.md`](multi-tenancy.md)) but because the chapter which matters here is `invite.chapter_id`, which the guard never sees — adding the guard would gate the redeemer's _current_ chapter instead, and 400 a brand-new user who has none. So `InviteService.redeem` loads `invite.chapter_id` and applies the guard's own hard-lock rule to it (`isSubscriptionHardLocked` in `#domain/constants/subscription-grace`): a `canceled` chapter, or a `past_due` one past the 3-day grace window, is refused with **403** and the token is **not** consumed — the same link works once the chapter recovers. This closed the one-day tail in which a token minted before a lapse could still add a member; a `past_due` chapter inside grace still admits, matching the guard. Minting stays gated at the guard (`@GraceBlocked`).
- Email delivery itself is optional infrastructure: with no `RESEND_API_KEY` configured, the API logs invite emails instead of sending them rather than failing the request — tokens are still created and can be shared as links. See `docs/internal/environment/ENV_REFERENCE.md` § Invite Email.
- If a user is already a member of the chapter and attempts to use an invite token for it, the API returns **409 Conflict**.
- **Redeeming needs the current Terms (#2302).** A user who hasn't accepted the Terms version the API enforces must send `accept_terms_privacy: true`, the join screen's checkbox. Without it the join is refused with **403** and the token is **not** consumed. The client recognises the refusal by its code, `legal.acceptance_required`, falling back to its message, `LEGAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED_MESSAGE`, for an API that predates #1020 and sends no code. The check runs after the 410, 409 and subscription-lock checks, so a dead link or a locked chapter says what is actually wrong. A user who already accepted joins without it. Rule and record: [`legal.md` § Acceptance record](legal.md#acceptance-record).
- **An invite link opened on a phone offers the app first (#4002).** On an iPhone or an Android phone (`phonePlatform` in `@repo/validation`; iPads and Android tablets get the web as before), web `/join` with an invite in its query offers the app first (its strings: [`writing.md` § Join chapter (pre-auth)](../ui/design-system/writing.md#join-chapter-pre-auth)): open it in the app (`frapp://join?token=…` on iOS; on Android a Chrome `intent:` link naming `live.frapp.mobile`, which opens Play when the app is missing), the platform's store listing, or continue in the browser, which goes on to the page as on a desktop. It shows signed in or out: `proxy.ts` lets a signed-out phone visitor carrying an invite reach `/join` instead of bouncing to `/sign-in`, and the page sends them on to sign-in only once they continue in the browser. That choice marks the URL (`web=1`), so the page doesn't offer the app again when sign-in brings them back. Joining on the web and then signing in to the app again is the double sign-in this removes. The app is offered to a signed-out visitor and to an account with no chapter. A member of a chapter keeps the web form, because the app sends a member with a chapter to its tabs and drops the invite, so the web is still where they join a second chapter. Nothing here carries the invite through an install. iOS has no way to without a third-party link service or a clipboard read. Android could pass it through the Play Store's install referrer, but that hands the invite to Google and needs a native change, so it isn't done (#4002). Either way the page tells a member who just installed to come back and tap Open in the app, where the link still holds the invite, and an invite emailed to the member is offered in the app after sign-in anyway (`GET /v1/invites/mine`, below). While the app is in TestFlight and Play internal testing only, the store buttons reach a listing only testers can install from, so a non-tester is told to continue in the browser. Once app links are live, the link opens an installed app before the page loads. That needs both halves: the dashboard origin publishes `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` and `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` for `/join` only (`apps/web/lib/app-links.ts`), from two `prod`-only values ([`ENV_REFERENCE.md` § References](../../docs/internal/environment/ENV_REFERENCE.md#references--framework-specific-names)), and a store build claims the same path. No store build claims it yet (#4002), so today every invite link opens the web. Only an invite is handed over: no other page, chapter creation included, sends a phone visitor to the app.
- **An invite can be read before it is used (#3352).** `POST /v1/invites/preview` takes the token in the body, like redeem, and returns the chapter's name and school, its text mark (resolved as [`branding.md` § Chapter mark](branding.md#chapter-mark) says, so never letters the chapter or its organization hides), its accent color, the inviter's display name, the token's role (the Member role the redeem may add beside it, as above, is not reported) and the expiry. It uses nothing up and answers only the 410s; the subscription lock, the Terms and an existing membership stay the redeem's to refuse. It is signed-in only, so a token opened before sign-in is previewed after it. Mobile's confirm card reads it. When the read fails with a 410, the card says the invite is dead and disables Join; when it fails any other way, the card shows the invite without the chapter, and the join still works.
- **Invites waiting for the account's verified address are offered without the link (#3776).** `GET /v1/invites/mine` (signed-in, no chapter needed) returns every invite emailed to the caller's address that is still pending (not used, revoked or expired), matched case-insensitively against `invites.email`, minus chapters the caller already belongs to, one per chapter (the newest), each with the preview's fields and its token. The address is the one Supabase Auth verified for the account (`email_confirmed_at` set, on the user `SupabaseAuthGuard` fetched with the caller's token), never one the client sends; an account without one gets an empty list. Returning the token to whoever proved they read that inbox reveals nothing the invite email didn't, and hosted projects require email confirmation ([`supabase.md` § Auth settings](../../docs/ops/deployment/supabase.md#auth-settings-hosted-dashboard-or-management-api)), asserted daily on both as `auth-email-confirm`. Joining one still goes through `POST /v1/invites/redeem` with its token, so every rule above applies. Matching is exact in the API, never a SQL `like`, where PostgREST reads `*` as a wildcard. Web `/join` offers them above the field, and mobile s02 on its question ([#3782](https://github.com/pdcarlson/Frapp/issues/3782)), each only when not opened with an invite link.
- **Redeem binds the signed-in user id, not the invite email.** `InviteService.redeem` takes the current `users.id` and the token. It does not require `users.email` to equal the address the invite was mailed to. Apple Hide My Email (`privaterelay.appleid.com`) therefore still joins when the member opens the invite after signing in.
- Clients redeem the opaque token, not the URL. Web `/join` and mobile s02 accept a pasted raw token, a full join URL (`…/join?token=…`, `frapp://join?token=…`), or the bare join URL the web Members invite panel's `Copy link` puts on the clipboard (until 2026-11-06 it was wrapped in role and expiry lines, which the parser still accepts) and extract via `extractInviteToken` in `@repo/validation` before `POST /v1/invites/redeem`. Opening `/join?token=`, `/join?invite=`, or `/join?code=` seeds the field (`extractInviteTokenFromQuery`; first non-empty key wins). Officers mint `?token=` (`mintJoinUrl` in `@repo/validation`, wrapped by the web/API/mobile `buildJoinUrl` / `webJoinUrl` helpers and the Members invite panel). Minted join URLs use `https:` — a public `http:` origin throws rather than putting the token on the wire (`assertHttpsJoinOrigin`). Loopback `http:` is allowed so local Infisical `APP_URL` still works. A production process must also mint/forward `https://app.frapp.live` (`assertProductionAppOrigin`): API boot when `SUPABASE_URL` is the `frapp-prod` project, landing `/join` and CTAs when `VERCEL_ENV=production`, and a set `EXPO_PUBLIC_APP_URL` on EAS production builds. Unset production `APP_URL` / `EXPO_PUBLIC_APP_URL` still falls back to that origin. The landing `/join` redirect uses the same origin rules ([`../product/surfaces.md`](../product/surfaces.md)).

## Chapter Directory Search

`GET /chapter-directory/search?q=...&university=...` returns up to 20 matching directory entries, used by the onboarding autocomplete wizard.

- `q`: matched against the organization's name, letters and nicknames (`org_aliases`: "FIJI"), the chapter's designation, and the school's full name, short name and nicknames (`aliases`: "RPI", "Rensselaer"). Both sides are normalized alike: case and accents aside ("manoa" finds Mānoa), apostrophes dropped ("st johns"), "&" read as "and" and "A&M" as "am" too. Every word of the query must start a word of the row, so a half-typed last word still matches ("phi gamma del"); joining words (and, at, of, the, in, for) are skipped ("sigma chi at ohio state"). A word of four or more letters that starts no word anywhere in the directory may instead be a close misspelling ("renselaer"); one that starts some word is taken as typed. Only the first eight words count, and a query with more than two words that start nothing finds nothing.
- Ranking: rows that matched every word as typed come first; then rows by how much the query names outright, the organization counted double, then the chapter, then the school, where a name (or any of its nicknames) counts when it is a phrase of the query or the query is the start of it, and a one-word chapter only in the second way; then closeness; then organization, school and chapter name. `search_chapter_directory` in `20261107043000_chapter_directory_search.sql` owns the details.
- `university`: narrows to rows where a word of the school's name, short name or a nickname starts with it ("rens", "RPI"). With `university` and no `q`, the call lists that school's chapters; with neither, it returns nothing.
- A directory row a chapter links but the seed no longer lists is retired: it keeps the link and is never returned.
- Response rows: `{ id, org_letters, org_name, archetype, chapter_designation, university, university_short, founded_year, default_colors, website }`.

## Onboarding Tutorial

When a new member joins a chapter (via invite token), they see a guided walkthrough on their first app launch.

### Walkthrough Screens

1. **Welcome** — Chapter name and logo (if uploaded).
2. **Chat** — Overview of channels, DMs, and announcements.
3. **Pick your chat display** — Cozy, Compact or Bubbles, the same three cards as Profile's Chat density, saved on this browser on the click. The browser's current display is preselected, which is Cozy on a browser that never picked one, so Next keeps it (#3787, 2026-11-10; [`chat/README.md` § Chat density](chat/README.md#chat-density)).
4. **Events** — Check in to earn points; never miss a meeting.
5. **Backwork** — Find study materials uploaded by chapter members.
6. **Study Hours** — Earn points by studying at approved locations.
7. **Points** — Balance, the chapter leaderboard, and the transaction log.
8. **Profile Setup** — Set display name and write a short bio. Photo upload is **mobile-only** today; the web half is tracked separately and the web slide does not promise it.
9. **Done** — CTA into chat, the landing surface on both web (`/chat`) and mobile. There is no web home screen; see [`chat/README.md`](chat/README.md).

This list said **seven** and omitted Points until the #920 Profile & pre-auth slice. The code has carried eight slides since `STEPS` was written, so the doc was stale rather than the implementation — behavior spec wins on what the product does, and here the shipped product *is* what it does. The display pick made it nine (2026-11-10). `apps/web/components/onboarding/onboarding-tutorial.spec.tsx` asserts the titles in order, so the two cannot drift apart again.

### Behavior

- The web tutorial can be skipped at any point via a "Skip" button. Mobile's s03 has no separate Skip: its buttons move on either way (#3352). The first-run tour that follows it has one on every stop.
- It can be revisited from settings (Profile > "Replay Tutorial").
- The walkthrough adapts to the surface: **web** is a modal slideshow (`apps/web/components/onboarding/onboarding-tutorial.tsx`). **Mobile** is s03 (`apps/mobile/app/(auth)/welcome.tsx`) and then the first-run tour over chat. s03 asks only what is missing, plus the display: a photo-and-name step when the member has no photo or no real name yet (the API stores `Member` when sign-in carried no name, as an Apple sign-up that hides its email does, and swaps it for the name Apple sends once it reaches the auth metadata), then `Pick your chat display` for every member (#3787: the same three cards as Settings → Chat density, the phone's current display preselected (Cozy on a phone that never picked one), saved on the tap), then a `Turn on notifications` step when the build can push and push is off ([`../ui/mobile/patterns.md`](../ui/mobile/patterns.md#push-notifications) § Push notifications), then chat. A member with both and push on sees only the display beat. There is no separate Skip: the notifications step's `Not now` and the display beat's `Go to chat` are how it is skipped (#3352). Canvas s03 is the drawing; the nine web slides are not restated as extra mobile cards.
- **First-run tour (mobile).** s03 opened with a static `You're in.` card (the chapter and its auto-joined channels) until 2026-11-07, when the owner asked for an interactive tour in place of card-based onboarding. Leaving s03 marks the tour due for that member (`markTourDue`, `apps/mobile/lib/onboarding/tour.ts`), and once Chat home's channel list has loaded it dims the real tab screen except one spot, three stops in order: the top channel section (`You're in <chapter>. Tap a channel to start talking.`), the tab bar's + (`Shortcuts live under the +. Hold it to pick your own.`), and the More tab (`The directory, your hours and settings are in More.`). A tap anywhere, or `Next`, moves on; the last stop reads `Done`; `Skip` sits in the top corner on every stop; Android's back steps back a stop, and on the first one skips. A stop whose control isn't on screen (no channels yet, or the list failed) is left out and the count says so (`1 of 2`). It ends for good on Skip or the last stop, on that phone; a member who joined before it shipped, or the officer who created the chapter (who never sees s03), never sees it. The spots are the real controls, marked with `useTourAnchor` (`apps/mobile/components/onboarding/tour.tsx`), so a redesign that moves a control moves its stop with it. Web has no tour; its slideshow above is unchanged.
- A `has_completed_onboarding` flag on the member record controls whether the tutorial is shown. On mobile the auth gate reads it from `GET /v1/chapters` and routes to s03 when the active membership's flag is false (`apps/mobile/lib/auth-gate.ts`). Leaving s03, by Go to chat or by any answer on its notifications step, PATCHes `PATCH /v1/members/me/onboarding`. Wizard-created memberships are written with the flag already true, so first officers skip s03. On mobile, setup itself carries what s03 would have: after create it shows the chapter's seeded public channels, then `Pick your chat display`, then the `Turn on notifications` step when the build can push and push is off, then the invite step (#3351). Both setups ask the founder for their chat display once the chapter exists (#4056), with the same three cards and the device's current display preselected (Cozy on a device that never picked one), so moving on keeps it: mobile as the step after the live moment, web as the wizard's `Pick your chat display` step between create and the invite step. Neither has a way back from it to before the chapter was made.
- An authenticated mobile session with **zero** memberships is routed to s02 (`(auth)/join.tsx`), which asks **How are you getting in?** (#3350). **I have an invite** opens the invite step there to redeem a token; **I'm starting our chapter** opens first-officer chapter creation, `(auth)/create-chapter` (#1102). An opened invite link skips the question.
