Motion sheet

First light, warm room

One signature moment on the crest, a warm field that rises with it and holds, hairlines that draw themselves in as you arrive, and chrome that answers the hand. Nothing loops on the page. Nothing moves the H1, the lead or the primary CTA. The demos below loop only so they can be watched.

1 · The signature moment

Demo loops every 6 seconds. On the page: once per load, motion-safe, mount-time; the mark paints whole from the first frame under reduced motion or without mask support.

Four layers on the mark's own subtree, composed as one gesture. Time runs left to right; the bar is 1,000 ms.

Wipe. A soft-edged mask uncovers the crest along its own 35-degree diagonal, base to crown. 520 ms, cubic-bezier(.45,.05,.15,1). mask-image only, never a fill change: the path stays #DDB844 and is whole at the end of every branch.

Settle. The mark rises 10 px and grows from 0.972 to 1 on a transform-only wrapper. 640 ms, the entrance curve. It never descends or overshoots, so it cannot read as a stamp or a seal.

Light. One radial of the mark's own gold at a 0.06 peak, 200% of the stage wide, rising once and holding. It is the ladder's lighter-surface elevation drawn soft, not a glow. The column clips it, so it never reaches the H1. If it cannot be seen, delete it; never raise it.

Rule. The column hairline scales in from the top, 300 ms at the standard curve, so the mark and the line it sits against arrive together. The inline border moves to a class so the pseudo-element can replace it.

5 · Tokens, and the one amendment

duration.micro 140

Kept. Focus ring.

duration.standard 220

Kept. Hover colour and border.

duration.context 300

Kept. Rule draws, card reveals, thread rows. The ceiling for anything below the fold.

easing.standard, .entrance, .exit

Kept unchanged from tokens.ts.

duration.signature 520

New, the amendment. A brand-mark reveal: one moment per page load, at most one play, at most three layers on the mark's own subtree, 700 ms ceiling per layer. Not a section entrance, so the 300 ceiling does not bind it.

easing.signature cubic-bezier(.45,.05,.15,1)

New. Light ease-in, long tail. The wipe and nothing else.

duration.signatureSettle 640

New, inside the signature class. Overruns the wipe so the two read as one gesture. Pairs with easing.entrance.

duration.ambientRise 660 · easing.ambient cubic-bezier(.45,0,.55,1)

New, inside the signature class and under its ceiling. A one-time luminance rise that ends at rest and holds. Never a loop.

stagger.step 60 · stagger.thread 80

New. Delay between siblings in a once-only reveal, in milliseconds, applied through an inline --i.

The amendment is a written change to design-system README section 7's table and a paragraph in foundations section 11, landing in the same commit as the stylesheet. If it is rejected, the crest pieces are cut, not trimmed to 300 ms: a 300 ms wipe is a different gesture. The mask and the light are page treatment, not mark design; brand-identity section 2's gradient ban scopes to mark exploration, and the mark's geometry and fill never change. That reading needs Paul's sign-off, which is decision D4 on the Spec sheet.

Later, behind the same wrapper: the check-in dot's bounded two-beat ring when the event frame presents the fact; the officer's reply rising into the thread with a typing row that then leaves the flow; a Check in confirm on the card.
