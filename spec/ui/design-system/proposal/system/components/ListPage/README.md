## Where each action goes

1. **View switch:** first after the title. On the phone, under the title.
2. **Search and Filter:** one control each. On the phone, the header button or one Filters chip.
3. **More:** export, settings and anything rare.
4. **The page's one main action:** last before close on web; the floating plus on the phone.
5. **Close:** the trailing corner on web; the back capsule on the phone.
6. **A group's own action** ("Show all", a sort): the section header's trailing edge, as quiet text.
7. **A row's one control**, only when it is the row's job (a task's check).
8. **Everything else about a row:** its menu.

## Rules

- Nothing sits between the header and the first group at rest.
- The body holds groups of rows and nothing else: no toolbar, no button on a line of its own.
- The list opens on the viewer's own items.
- At zero, the group stays and holds one line with the first action at its end.
- No filled button on a list page.

## You provide

The title, the view options, the filters, the main action, and the groups.
