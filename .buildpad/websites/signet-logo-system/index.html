01

The mark

Two renderings of the same idea, built for different surfaces. Solid is the S filled in gold — used anywhere the mark sits on an existing dark surface (navbars, favicons, dark app icon). Impression is the S cut out of a solid gold field as negative space, as if pressed into it — used wherever the icon needs its own opaque field (light app icon, avatars, merch).

Solid
navbar · favicon · dark icon · single color
Impression
light app icon · avatars · merch · stamps
Construction
Geometric sans letterform, one dominant idea, generous internal negative space so it doesn't clog at tiny sizes.
Fixed, not per-tenant
Only one icon exists across every chapter. The mark carries the Signet brand; the chapter accent lives entirely in UI tokens.
What it avoids
No blue, no serif, no checkmark, no literal ring or wax seal, no gradient/hexagon/swirl "generic AI" vocabulary.

08

Do / don't

Do
  • · Keep the mark gold on every tenant, regardless of chapter accent
  • · Use Solid on dark surfaces, Impression where an opaque field is needed
  • · Keep clear space of at least ¼ the mark's height on all sides
  • · Use the flat/no-bevel version for anything under ~32px
Don't
  • · Recolor the mark to a chapter's accent color, ever
  • · Add a checkmark, ring, wax-seal texture, or gradient
  • · Rotate, skew, add a drop shadow, or outline the mark
  • · Pair it with a serif wordmark or a blue background
