# Brand identity: decided

Name: **Signet** (replaces "Frapp"). Signet ring = fraternal seal/identity, and "signet" maps to the AI differentiator: a trusted, sourced answer from your chapter's own records.

Tagline: **"Ask your chapter anything."** Outcome-led, points straight at the AI/RAG differentiator, no "AI-powered" buzzwords.

Positioning: AI-first OS for Greek life, competing on owning the AI/RAG whitespace (nobody else does AI search over chapter docs/chat/meeting history) rather than "modern UI" (already crowded by Dueflow, MyGreek, Greekly, GreekDash, OurHouse).
